The first version does not have to be the last version. That would be a terrible rule for the internet.

With [Spacefast](https://spacefast.com/), republishing updates the same link. Each change saves a version, and you can restore a version still in your history.

## Give the next idea a turn

Fix a sentence. Add a page. Make the button less mysterious. Publish again when you are ready. Your link can stay put while the work grows.
