Your agent made a website. You like it. Now it is sitting in a folder, waving at nobody.

Give it a home on [Spacefast](https://spacefast.com/). Ask your agent to publish, then choose who can see it. New spaces are private by default.

## A real next step

A portfolio, a club page, a ridiculous little game: the internet has room for it. Spacefast supports static sites and supported full-stack apps. Check the [docs](https://spacefast.com/docs) for the publishing path your project needs.
