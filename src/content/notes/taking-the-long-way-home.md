Some things are for your friends. Some things are for your team. Some things are for every person who has ever searched for a tiny frog website.

[Spacefast](https://spacefast.com/) starts spaces private. Invite people, create a viewer link, or make a space public when that is the right choice.

## Let the idea find its people

A private URL alone does not grant access. Choose the sharing setting before sending someone a link. Search indexing is a separate setting. You get to decide when the curtain opens.
