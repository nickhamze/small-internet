A field guide to neighborhood birds. A catalog of suspicious mugs. A fan page for one particularly good sandwich.

Ask your agent to turn your favorite subject into a small website. Add the details that only you would notice. Then publish it with [Spacefast](https://spacefast.com/).

## Start with one useful page

Explain the subject, give visitors something to explore, and make the next click obvious. This is an idea to build, not a downloadable finished guide.
