Books for rainy days. Tools your club uses. Movies with truly excellent opening credits.

Turn the list you keep sending people into a website. Let your agent help with the structure, then add your own reasons for every pick.

## Give it an address

Publish the site with [Spacefast](https://spacefast.com/). Choose its audience and keep updating it at the same link. This is a starting idea, not a ready-made app.
